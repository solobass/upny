# UPNY Ventures — LP Ask Script
### Verbal Pitch · 2–3 Minutes · Angels / HNWIs / Family Offices

---

> **How to use this script:**
> This is a spoken script, not a document to hand someone. Read it out loud until it sounds like *you*, not a pitch. The brackets are pacing and tone cues — internalize them, don't say them. The goal by the end is a clear, confident ask — not a conversation that trails off.

---

## THE SCRIPT

---

**[HOOK — Lead with the data. Calm, confident. Let the numbers do the work.]**

"New York State has spent billions building one of the best deep-tech research pipelines in the country. Energy, semiconductors, nano-tech, quantum, advanced materials — coming out of Cornell, RPI, SUNY, Albany, Clarkson. World-class science. Real problems being solved.

And here's a number that tells you everything: Launch NY — the state's primary startup readiness organization — prepares over 350 companies a year to become investment-ready. Last year, they deployed capital into 36 of them.

That's the gap."

---

**[THE PROBLEM — Let this land. Don't rush it.]**

"Every year these founders go to pitch competitions and demo days. The science is solid. The technology is defensible. And they keep hearing the same thing:

*'Not VC ready. No customers. No revenue.'*

The assumption is that these companies are too early. But that's not right. Cornell's commercialization program shows a 42-to-1 follow-on funding leverage ratio — meaning when founders get structured commercial support, capital follows at scale. NSF's I-Corps program, which is built almost entirely around customer discovery and commercialization, has produced 1,400 startups that collectively raised $3.16 billion.

The science isn't the problem. The commercialization layer is what's missing. And in Upstate New York, at pre-seed — nobody is purpose-built to provide it."

---

**[THE SOLUTION — Pick up energy here. This is the pivot.]**

"That's the gap UPNY was built to fill.

We're a pre-seed venture fund. We come in before the customers, before the revenue — and we bring both capital and a structured go-to-market program to get them there. We run founders through a four-phase commercialization engine: customer discovery, pipeline building, value proposition testing. The goal is real commercial proof — a signed pilot, a letter of intent, an early paying customer — before we write the next check.

NYSERDA's research on New York cleantech incubators found that nearly 40% of founders said access to capital was their number one challenge. Not the technology. Capital, paired with commercialization support. That's exactly what we provide.

We're targeting 20 companies in Fund I. Five million dollars. First institutional capital for founders who've been through the university system and are ready to build companies."

---

**[WHY ME — Personal, grounded. Not a resume recitation.]**

"I've spent twenty years scaling software companies. I've built and run accelerator programs. I grew up off-grid — wind turbines on the property — so this isn't an abstract thesis for me. I've been sitting in these pitch rooms for years watching brilliant founders walk away without a check.

UPNY is the investor I kept waiting for someone else to build."

---

**[THE ASK — Direct. Specific. No trailing off.]**

"We're currently building our LP base for Fund I. I'm not asking you to commit today — I'm asking for a thirty-minute conversation to walk you through the thesis, the structure, and the deal flow. If it fits where you're deploying capital right now, great. If it doesn't, I'd still value your perspective.

Can we find thirty minutes?"

---

## HANDLING THE MOST COMMON RESPONSES

---

**"It's too early / too risky."**
> "That's exactly the point — and the data actually reduces that concern. Cornell's commercialization program shows a 42-to-1 follow-on funding leverage ratio when founders get structured support. We don't follow on without commercial proof. The GTM program is the de-risking mechanism."

---

**"I don't know the Upstate NY market."**
> "Most LPs don't — which is why the sourcing edge is real. Launch NY alone prepares 350+ companies per year for investment readiness. That's a consistent, institutional-grade pipeline almost no outside investor has direct access to. We do."

---

**"What's the return model?"**
> "Pre-seed checks into companies that raise Seed and Series A rounds within 18–36 months. The GTM program accelerates the commercial proof that makes the next round possible. NSF's I-Corps — which runs an almost identical commercialization model — has produced startups that collectively raised $3.16 billion in follow-on funding. We're not holding indefinitely; we're building fundable companies with a clear path forward."

---

**"How are you different from an accelerator?"**
> "Accelerators take equity for programs. We're a fund — we invest capital and provide the commercial support as an integrated part of how we operate, not as a product we sell. Our LPs are invested in the portfolio, not in a service business."

---

**"How are you different from Launch NY?"**
> "Launch NY is doing important work — they're the ones preparing 350+ companies a year for investment readiness. We come in right after that. We're the capital and commercialization layer for the founders who've gone through that process and are ready for their first institutional check. We're complementary to the ecosystem, not competing with it."
